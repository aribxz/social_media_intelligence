About SMI

SMI (Social Media Intelligence) is a research prototype built for the SIH hackathon internal round. It analyzes a sample of public Twitter data — 15,000 tweets across an 81-minute window — to surface propagation, sentiment and trend signals. Sentiment labels are model-predicted, not ground truth.
